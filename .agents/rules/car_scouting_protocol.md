# Vehicle Scouting & Market Recommendation Protocol

Whenever analyzing, recommending, or evaluating any vehicle deal for flip or purchase in Sri Lanka, you MUST strictly adhere to the following 5-step protocol:

---

## 1. Full-Spectrum Price Floor Search (No Artificial Lower Clamps)
- Never restrict searches with narrow lower price filters (e.g. if the user says "budget 5M-6M", do NOT filter min_price=4.5M).
- Always sweep from the absolute market baseline (Rs. 200,000+) to capture all lower-priced, distress-sale, or manual variants of that model.
- Find the **absolute lowest-priced active listing** in the country for that specific make, model, and year range.

---

## 2. Transmission & Spec Disaggregation
Always separate and explicitly label:
- **Transmission**: Manual vs. Automatic (Manuals trade 15%–25% lower in Sri Lanka with different buyer profiles).
- **Origin / Spec**: Japanese vs. Indian / Regional variants (e.g. Japanese Swift Beetle vs. Indian Maruti Swift Dzire, Toyota Vitz vs. Toyota Etios).
- **Engine / Fuel**: Petrol vs. Diesel CRDi vs. Hybrid.

---

## 3. Mandatory Comparative "Floor vs. Recommendation" Analysis
For any recommended car, you must compare it directly against the lowest-priced option on the market:
1. State the **Cheapest Listing Found** (Price, Transmission, Mileage, Location, Link).
2. Compare why the recommended vehicle is better (e.g., Automatic vs Manual, lower mileage, closer location, better registration series `WP CAx` vs `WP KAx`, genuine Japanese spec vs regional).
3. Show the net profit margin differences between flipping the cheapest baseline vs. the premium clean variant.

---

## 4. Full Flip Math & Detailing Breakdown
Every deal evaluation must include:
- **Agreed / Negotiated Buy Target**
- **Detailing & Restoration Costs** (Cut & polish, paint correction, interior steam clean)
- **Mechanical Service & Suspension Prep**
- **Transfer / Transport Costs**
- **Realistic Resale Price Target** (positioned below market ceiling for fast 7–14 day liquidity)
- **Estimated Net Profit (LKR) & ROI (%)**

---

## 5. Specific Pre-Purchase Inspection Gotchas
Provide 3–5 model-specific mechanical, electrical, or chassis inspection points tailored to the Sri Lankan climate (e.g., coastal rust, AC cooling coils, hybrid battery health, steering column couplers, gearbox shift delay).
